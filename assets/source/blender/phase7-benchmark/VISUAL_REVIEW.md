# Phase 7 Benchmark Visual Review

Status: **NOT APPROVED — TECHNICAL PIPELINE BENCHMARK ONLY**

Review date: 2026-07-20  
Reviewer: product owner

The current character proves that the scripted Blender build, armature/action,
GLB export, provenance record, and deterministic benchmark render path work.
It does not establish the target player-model quality. The visible result is
too blocky and shape-like and must not be reused as the approved hero style.

## Required next visual pass

- Establish a convincing human tactical silhouette with deliberate anatomy and
  proportion instead of assembled primitive volumes.
- Use layered construction: undersuit or soft goods, fitted armor shells,
  straps/seams, gloves, boots, and functional attachment points.
- Replace hard box transitions with controlled curvature, bevel hierarchy, and
  designed panel flow while retaining a readable stylized profile.
- Resolve hands, trigger/grip contact, shoulder stock contact, and weapon scale.
- Give the helmet, torso, limbs, and rifle distinct primary/secondary/tertiary
  forms that remain readable at tactical distance.
- Keep color blocking purposeful and faction-readable without relying on color
  alone; validate the silhouette in grayscale.
- Produce a deformation-capable skin, first-person arms, required gameplay
  clips, LODs, and outline/contact tests before any G6 review.

## Acceptance evidence for the replacement

Capture close, tactical-mid, and far views; front/side/back orthographic checks;
neutral and action poses; grayscale and selected color-vision simulations; and
low/high quality renders. Include wireframe/topology, skin-weight stress poses,
weapon-contact views, GLB validation, manifests/provenance, and 2/4/8-character
performance captures. The current benchmark remains preserved as pipeline
evidence and as a negative visual baseline.
