# V6-C Visual Benchmark Rev5 Review

Status: **MATERIALLY IMPROVED SILHOUETTE / VISUALLY SELF-REJECTED FOR V6-C / NOT G6**

Rev5 removes every capsule-like rev4 limb shell and replaces them with thin surfaces derived directly from accepted V6-A anatomy. The silhouette is materially cleaner, the rifle no longer presents as a broad white toy shell, and the undersuit reads as a continuous garment.

Visual rejection reasons:

- Raw selected-topology boundaries produce visible pixel-stepped edges on the chest, shoulders, and limbs.
- Broad white limb inlays create blocky cross-like graphic patches instead of narrow integrated routes.
- The two rear helmet plates read as rectangular attachments.
- The rifle finish remains too bright and reflective despite the gunmetal remap.

Rev5 is preserved byte-for-byte. Rev6 is limited to smoothing those fitted surfaces, reducing limb ceramic to narrow splines, simplifying the rear helmet, and darkening the rifle. No V6-C or G6 acceptance is claimed.
