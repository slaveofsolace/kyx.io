# V6-C visual benchmark revision 1 direct review

Status: **SELF-REJECTED / PRESERVED FAILURE / NOT V6-C / NOT G6**

Revision 1 is a clean rebuild from the pinned accepted V6-A anatomy and does
not inherit any V6-B visible construction. It establishes a continuous fitted
undersuit, a new helmet assembly, new fitted and swept armor surfaces, new boot
lofts, and a new continuous-profile Auto Rifle. Direct, component, and true-
perspective distance evidence is preserved with the Blend.

The direct renders nevertheless fail the required non-blocky visual bar:

- the helmet reads cleanly from the front, but its symmetric full shell expands
  into an oversized round dome from the side and rear, while the visor projects
  too far ahead of the shell;
- the paired chest patches remain too broad and breast-cup-like, develop sharp
  lower tips, and still combine into a white bib read instead of smaller
  layered clavicle and rib protection;
- the deltoid shells flare away from the body like horns;
- forearm, thigh, knee, and shin shells are curved in depth but retain nearly
  parallel front-view edges, so they still read as isolated rounded rectangles;
- the boot upper and instep volume are improved, but the accepted source feet
  protrude laterally because the boot envelope was centered too far inward;
  the white dorsal pieces consequently still read like applied toe badges;
- the rifle is one connected side profile, but that solution creates a large
  flat beveled slab with insufficient receiver, stock, grip, and handguard
  sectional development.

Revision 1 is preserved and must not unlock rigging, contact, retopology, UVs,
export, runtime integration, V6-C, or G6. Revision 2 must start from V6-A again,
use measured foot and head bounds, aggressively taper limb-shell perimeters,
split chest protection into smaller layers, replace the full helmet orb with a
tight dark liner and open ivory crown, and build the rifle from overlapping
continuous three-dimensional lofts rather than a single side-profile slab.
