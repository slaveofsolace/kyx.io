# V6-C Visual Benchmark Revision 9 - Direct Review

## Decision

**SELF-REJECTED / NOT ACCEPTED / NOT V6-C / NOT G6**

Revision 9 is a substantial structural improvement over revisions 7 and 8, but it does not yet meet the pinned production-form target. The character still reads as a clean procedural armor prototype rather than an authored production model. Per the gate contract, no retopology, rigging, export, runtime integration, or later acceptance work may advance from this revision.

## Direct visual findings

- The silhouette is narrower and more athletic, but it remains generic at 5 m and collapses to sparse white pads over a black body at 20 m and 40 m.
- The helmet is sealed and wrapped, but the oversized smooth dome, flat face-wide visor, simple side discs, and thin external curves do not achieve the target's nested crown, brow, cheek, jaw, ear, and neck architecture.
- The torso now conforms to the accepted V6-A anatomy and the rev8 box/donut construction is gone. However, the broad pale chest shell, long flat sternum strip, and shallow blue abdomen panels still read as large unarticulated surfaces rather than interlocking hard-surface assemblies.
- The shoulder cups remain detached triangular/flared forms. Upper-arm, forearm, thigh, knee, and shin protection still reads as spaced cylindrical cuffs and rectangular pads rather than connected compound shells with designed flex transitions.
- The pelvis and upper legs expose too much uninterrupted black undersuit. The hip-to-thigh anchors are not visually strong enough to establish a coherent armored lower-body system.
- The boots are more angular than rev8, but the last, outsole, toe cap, ankle upper, and heel counter remain simplified wedge volumes and do not yet match the target's mechanically layered boot construction.
- The darker value hierarchy is improved, but the bright pearl/cyan glossy response is still toy-like and lacks authored roughness breakup, woven material definition, panel-edge wear, fasteners, vents, and small functional detail.
- The rifle is the strongest component: it has multi-depth receiver/handguard volumes, an open stock, and real cutouts. Its trigger group, pistol grip, magazine, and local mechanical layering still need another authored detail pass, and no held-contact evidence is claimed.

## Improvements preserved in rev9

- Built directly from the accepted V6-A anatomy source; no rev8 geometry was used.
- Replaced the rev8 oversized box torso and donut waist with body-conforming torso panels and a continuous waist wrap.
- Established a continuous dark undersuit, narrower silhouette, sealed helmet, more angular boot baseline, and a substantially more credible rifle.
- Added nested under-shell/ceramic construction intent, darker value grouping, authored seam material assignment, and three future contact-witness empties.

## Required next visual strike

Freeze rev9 as evidence and create a distinct revision 10 from accepted V6-A plus the pinned production concept, without using rev9 geometry as the production source.

1. Rebuild the helmet as a compact interlocking assembly with layered crown, brow, cheek, jaw, temple, occipital, and neck components; eliminate the smooth dome and planar shield read.
2. Replace the broad chest shell and central strip with a connected clavicle/sternum/rib/abdomen panel system that uses controlled overlap, thickness, recesses, fasteners, and flex interfaces.
3. Rebuild shoulder, arm, hip, thigh, knee, and shin protection as continuous nested volumes designed around joints; eliminate floating cups, cuffs, and rectangular pad language.
4. Establish a fully authored lower-body armor rhythm and a mechanically layered boot with clear outsole, toe, instep, ankle, heel, and articulation logic.
5. Add production material breakup and functional micro-detail while preserving the dark/light hierarchy and readable cyan/coral accents.
6. Give the rifle one refinement pass, then render a new direct, component, distance, and pinned-reference packet for direct visual review.
