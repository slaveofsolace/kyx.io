# V6-B production-sculpt revision 11 direct visual review

Status: **SELF-REJECTED / PRESERVED FAILURE / NOT V6-B / NOT G6**

Revision 11 is a clean, isolated departure from the earlier predicate-cut plate
lane. It proves that the accepted V6-A anatomy can remain the visible continuous
technical garment while authored quad strips, lofted boot lasts, a connected
helmet assembly, and a volumetric rifle are built around it. The pinned V6-A
input remained unchanged and the Blend plus nine renders are preserved here.

Direct inspection rejects the candidate for the following visible defects:

- shoulder shells flare upward and outward like horns rather than fitted
  deltoid protection;
- forearm, thigh, knee, and shin shells still read as isolated rectangular
  guards at front and gameplay distance;
- the waist harness is visibly wider than the body and reads as a floating hoop;
- the helmet crown is too round, remains open at the top, and the cheek rails
  are over-wide side blocks;
- the accepted body head shows through the helmet opening and the body feet show
  behind the boot lasts from the rear;
- boot lasts have better heel-to-toe volume than rev10, but the shafts are too
  cylindrical and the toe bridges still read as applied badges;
- the rifle has real depth and a tapered handguard, but the side presentation
  remains too planar, the grip and magazine silhouettes are insufficiently
  differentiated, and the review framing clips the stock.

Revision 11 must not unlock V6-C, rigging, export, runtime integration, contact,
or G6. Revision 12 must change the silhouette and construction grammar: one
coherent thoracic bib, fitted projected limb shields, a lower closed angular
helmet, hidden head/foot underlayers, a body-close waist treatment, and a
stronger three-dimensional rifle presentation.
