# V6-B production-sculpt revision 13 direct visual review

Status: **SELF-REJECTED / PRESERVED FAILURE / NOT V6-B / NOT G6**

Revision 13 removes the failed body-projection construction from rev12 and
replaces it with bounded authored guards. The stretched shards are gone, the
accepted V6-A body remains the continuous technical-garment underlayer, the
head and feet remain hidden by equipment, and the helmet, boot, and rifle
assemblies are intact.

Direct inspection nevertheless rejects the revision because the scaled fitted
waist-harness object inherited a world-space center Z of approximately 0.744 m.
That places the horizontal harness across the crotch instead of the waist. The
error is visible in the full views and breaks the intended torso-to-pelvis
construction regardless of the otherwise clean bounded geometry.

Revision 13 is preserved byte-for-byte and must not unlock V6-C, rigging,
export, runtime integration, contact, or G6. Revision 14 may only restore the
harness world Z while preserving its X/Y fit, reduce the squared heel cups, and
add one restrained crown seam.
