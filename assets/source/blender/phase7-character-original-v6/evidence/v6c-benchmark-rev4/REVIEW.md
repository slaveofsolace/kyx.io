# V6-C Visual Benchmark Rev4 Review

Status: **TECHNICALLY CLEAN / VISUALLY SELF-REJECTED FOR V6-C / NOT G6**

Rev4 removes rev3's paired lower-rib cup patches, improves material separation, flattens the helmet liner, and makes the rifle more legible. Its nested graphite/ceramic limb construction is also cleaner than rev3.

Visual rejection reasons:

- The shoulder, forearm, thigh, knee, and shin protection still changes the silhouette as isolated capsule-like pods.
- The rear helmet remains too featureless and orb-like despite the flatter measured profile.
- The boots remain smooth and visually heavy at the ankle and toe.
- The broad bright rifle flow shell still reads more like a toy shell than a production weapon receiver.

Rev4 is preserved byte-for-byte. Rev5 replaces the capsule geometry with low-profile surfaces copied from the accepted V6-A anatomy. No V6-C or G6 acceptance is claimed.
