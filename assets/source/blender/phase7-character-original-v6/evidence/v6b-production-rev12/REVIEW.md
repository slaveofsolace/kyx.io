# V6-B production-sculpt revision 12 direct visual review

Status: **SELF-REJECTED / PRESERVED FAILURE / NOT V6-B / NOT G6**

Revision 12 materially improves the rev11 thoracic, cowl, shoulder, helmet,
underlayer, boot, and rifle direction. The one-piece curved bib is more coherent,
the source head and feet are hidden beneath authored equipment, the shoulder
saddle is smaller, the crown is closed, and the rifle now has a dark volumetric
handguard core with differentiated grip and magazine silhouettes.

The full and detail plates nevertheless expose a hard technical failure: several
body-projected limb strips jumped between disconnected body surfaces. Their
subdivision and solidification produced long stretched shards through the waist,
arms, and legs. Measured object bounds identify the left forearm, both thigh,
knee, and related projection results as invalid. The failure is visible in every
full view and invalidates the revision regardless of the improved unaffected
areas.

Revision 12 is preserved byte-for-byte and must not unlock V6-C, rigging,
export, runtime integration, contact, or G6. The next revision must remove the
limb projection path entirely, replace it with bounded authored curved shields,
pull the waist treatment into the body, close the rear boot silhouette with heel
cups, and retain only the unaffected improvements.
