# V6-C Visual Benchmark Rev3 Review

Status: **STRONGEST PRESERVED NEW-LANE CANDIDATE / VISUALLY SELF-REJECTED FOR V6-C / NOT G6**

Rev3 is technically clean and fixes rev2's invalid chest shard, exposed source feet, shoulder knobs, rear helmet band, and overlong rifle. It remains preserved byte-for-byte as the strongest pre-rev4 reference.

Visual rejection reasons:

- The graphite lower-rib patches still read as paired cup forms rather than fitted tailoring or protection.
- Forearm, thigh, patella, and tibia ceramic pieces still read as isolated oval pads even with center seams.
- The dark helmet liner remains too smooth and generically orb-like from side and rear views.
- The boots are coherent and enclosing, but still too smooth and visually oversized.
- The continuous rifle is materially better than the original slab, but remains too sparse and toy-like for production acceptance.

No V6-C or G6 acceptance is claimed. No rig, retopology, UV, texture-bake, animation, LOD, GLB, runtime, or performance work is authorized from this visual rejection.
