# V6-B0 proof-of-method revision 1 strict self-review

Date: 2026-07-22

Decision: **SELF-REJECTED / PRESERVED METHOD FAILURE / NOT V6-B0 APPROVED / NOT V6-B / NOT V6-C / NOT G6**

Revision 1 was the first bounded render of a production-method study made from the immutable continuous V6-A anatomy. It was stopped immediately after direct visual inspection. It was not expanded into a full body.

## Exact blockers

- The chest and deltoid surface selections break into noisy disconnected islands, so neither a continuous manufactured perimeter nor a seated armor assembly is visible.
- Boundary-derived trim follows raw selection edges as a jagged wire. It reads as procedural noise rather than a sealed manufactured edge.
- The raised stock bridge is an oversized slab that dominates the bust and repeats the user's original blocky/shape-like complaint.
- Lighting washes dark fabric, glove, rubber, and graphite toward the same pale value, hiding material hierarchy.
- The hand view shows five digits, but it does not prove four fingers wrapped around the grip, the index finger inside the guard on the trigger, or unobstructed palm contact.
- The stock/shoulder termination is visually hidden by the slab and armor clutter.
- The source-eye witnesses remained visible as two floating spheres, which invalidates the presentation framing.
