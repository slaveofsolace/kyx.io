# V6-C visual benchmark revision 2 direct review

Status: **SELF-REJECTED / PRESERVED FAILURE / NOT V6-C / NOT G6**

Revision 2 starts from the accepted V6-A scene again and only reads revision 1
as an integrity pin. It materially improves the measured boot envelope, removes
the exposed source toes, replaces the rifle slab with continuous three-
dimensional path lofts, tightens the helmet rear volume, and produces more
strongly tapered limb leaves.

Direct inspection still rejects the candidate:

- the left lower-rib fitted patch develops an out-of-family 0.436 m span and
  produces a long dark shard through the chest; the paired lower layers are
  therefore technically and visually invalid;
- the upper chest pieces remain too breast-cup-like and do not yet form a
  restrained layered clavicle/rib assembly;
- aggressive shoulder taper collapses both deltoid shells into round primitive
  knobs rather than fitted armor;
- the limb pieces are no longer rectangular, but remain visually isolated
  white pads with insufficient integration into suit seam logic;
- the dark helmet liner is tighter than revision 1, but the low starting ring
  of the ivory open crown becomes a horizontal rear band;
- the new rifle has coherent continuous depth, grip, magazine, stock, and
  handguard flow, but remains too long and visually under-articulated.

Revision 2 is preserved byte-for-byte and must not unlock rigging, contact,
retopology, UVs, export, runtime integration, V6-C, or G6. Revision 3 may remain
inside the new V6-C lane, remove the invalid fitted chest layers, replace them
with bounded high-density no-subdivision patches, rebuild the shoulder shells,
raise and simplify the crown cap, and shorten/detail the rifle.
