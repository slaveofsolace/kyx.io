# V6-B production-sculpt revision 14 direct visual review

Status: **STRONGEST CURRENT BOUNDED CANDIDATE / VISUALLY SELF-REJECTED FOR V6-B / NOT G6**

Revision 14 performs only the authorized correction to preserved revision 13.
The fitted waist harness is restored from world Z 0.744483 m to 1.034004 m
without changing its X/Y center or span, both heel cups are reduced, and one
restrained crown-keel curve is added. Direct front, side, back, three-quarter,
helmet, chest, limb, boot, and rifle renders plus true-perspective 5 m, 20 m,
and 40 m renders were inspected. The deterministic waist error is fixed, the
stretched-shard failure remains absent, and the heel blocks are smaller.

The revision is the cleanest and most coherent candidate in this isolated lane,
but it does not yet clear the requested production-sculpt or non-blocky visual
bar:

- the helmet is enclosed and coherent, but its dome, thick cheek rails, and
  visor assembly still read as assembled hard primitives; the crown keel is a
  restrained accent rather than a meaningful form refinement;
- the single thoracic bib is continuous, but it remains oversized, broad, and
  visually flat, with insufficient edge transitions into the garment;
- forearm, thigh, knee, and shin protection no longer jumps surfaces, but the
  pieces still read as isolated rectangular appliques rather than integrated
  fitted armor;
- the boots enclose the source feet and have reduced heel cups, but the shafts,
  soles, and toe badges remain simple blockout forms without a convincing
  articulated boot construction;
- the rifle is volumetric and has differentiated receiver, grip, magazine,
  stock, and barrel regions, yet their silhouettes and surface transitions are
  still visibly blockout-level;
- at true-perspective gameplay distances the human silhouette survives, but
  the character-specific equipment language loses distinction before it reads
  as a finished production asset.

Revision 14 is therefore the strongest artifact to hand to an independent
visual reviewer, not a V6-B acceptance. Further progress requires an authorized
surface-and-topology redesign pass rather than another bounded transform tweak.
It must not unlock V6-C, rigging, contact, retopology, UVs, export, runtime
integration, or G6.
