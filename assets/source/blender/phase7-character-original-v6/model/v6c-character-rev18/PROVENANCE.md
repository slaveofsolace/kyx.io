# KYX.IO Rev18 Character Remediation Provenance

Status: candidate-only; human visual, runtime-camera, and project legal review
remain required.

Rev18 is a repository-authored remediation of the repository-owned Rev17
master. The exact Rev17 input remained byte-identical at SHA-256
`832f85562aacdface4d05d86158f9769a69572c99f3f22f98908b30cf986f7c8`.
The revision was authored through
`../../scripts/author_remediate_v6c_character_rev18.py` and preserved in
`kyx-v6c-character-rev18-master.blend`.

The script reshapes the inherited body proportions, removes floating lower-leg
plates, authors fitted lower-body and boot surfaces, tightens armor and helmet
pieces, replaces the icy palette with a dark graphite hierarchy, adds the
`socket_melee` hand socket, and authors a sampled 24-frame melee action. The
melee blade exists only in the separate proof export and is derived from the
right-hand socket; it is not part of the normal LOD or first-person exports.

No downloaded meshes, third-party character bases, third-party rigs, external
motion-capture clips, marketplace assets, private license packs, or ripped game
content were introduced. Blender's built-in primitives and operators were used
as authoring tools.

This record documents production provenance only. It does not grant G6,
release eligibility, default promotion, legal approval, or human visual
acceptance.
