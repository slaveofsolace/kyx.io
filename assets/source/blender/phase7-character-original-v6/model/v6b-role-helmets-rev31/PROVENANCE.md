# KYX V6B Rev31 role helmet family provenance

Status: runtime candidate only; human visual and in-game acceptance pending.

## Source lineage

Rev31 was authored from the tracked Rev24 runtime master, SHA-256
`1d891ff9ddcfef400a812f030c1a7283af5c866e3cd72a17d5b341b6f9bafbfe`.
Rev24 is the animated browser-runtime descendant of the slimmer Material Flow
v9 presentation body. The source remained byte-identical through authoring.

The anatomy lineage originates in Blender Human Base Meshes v1.4.1, specifically
the sanitized `Body Male - Realistic` selection. Blender Studio / Blender
Foundation and community contributors distribute that anatomy under CC0 1.0.
The governing evidence and license snapshot remain in:

- `assets/source/blender/phase7-character-original-v6/PROVENANCE.md`
- `assets/source/blender/phase7-character-original-v6/LICENSES/CC0-1.0.txt`

The technical garment, fitted segmented armor language, gloves, compact
footwear pass, Rev17-compatible rig/actions/sockets, abdomen continuity bridge,
and all four Rev31 helmet variants are project-authored derivatives.

## Rev31 authored changes

- removed the rejected Rev24 egg/bubble helmet geometry from every retained LOD
  source mesh;
- authored a closed cyber-cowl, tapered faceted shell, crown, faceplate, visor,
  chin, temples, cheeks, mouth vent, and neck seal as one manufacturing family;
- added exact role variants keyed `assault`, `breacher`, `recon`, and `duelist`;
- tightened footwear by 6 percent in width and 9 percent fore-aft;
- removed the inherited floor witness geometry;
- replaced the disconnected waist presentation with a fitted flex gasket and
  three overlapping articulated abdomen bands;
- retained the 66-bone rig and all 16 imported Rev17 TP/FP clips.

No mesh, texture, animation, model, or code was copied from
`NotHereButAfk/Ev.io` or ev.io. The restrained helmet prototype and earlier
Khronos SciFiHelmet study were visual references only; neither source mesh was
shipped in Rev31.
