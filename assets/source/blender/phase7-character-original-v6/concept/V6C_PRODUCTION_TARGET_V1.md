# KYX Vanguard V6-C production target v1

## Status and use

This is a pinned visual construction reference for the next Blender benchmark.
It is not geometry, a runtime asset, V6-C acceptance, or G6 acceptance.

- Generation path: built-in image-generation tool
- Source references: V6-C rev7 direct-review board and rifle render

The accepted V6-A anatomy remains authoritative. The generated sheet is used
for armor, helmet, materials, boot, and rifle construction language only; its
body bulk and proportions do not override V6-A.

## Required construction language

- One coherent tapered torso cuirass/harness with interlocking clavicle,
  sternum, rib, waist, and back-spine plates.
- Functional overlap, bevel, seam, fastener, vent, and thickness hierarchy.
- Anatomically fitted shoulder, elbow, forearm, thigh, knee, shin, ankle, and
  boot transitions with woven undersuit retained in flex zones.
- Low-profile faceted helmet crown, framed visor, brow, chin/jaw, temple
  modules, and a structured rear vent housing.
- Credible compact Auto Rifle construction: stock, receiver, magazine well,
  ejection area, trigger guard, pistol grip, forward support-hand zone,
  handguard, barrel, muzzle device, rails, vents, and manufacturing seams.
- Charcoal woven suit, satin gunmetal and warm pearl ceramic armor, restrained
  cyan telemetry light, and minimal coral safety accents.
- A readable silhouette at 5 m, 20 m, and 40 m and geometry that remains
  feasible for retopology, rigging, first-person arms, LODs, and two-hand
  weapon contact.

## Prohibited regressions

- No separate breast-cup plates, floating foam patches, decorative cable arcs,
  primitive capsules/tubes, square block pads, broad featureless surfaces,
  egg/sphere helmet, smooth toy plastic, exposed skin, high heels, cape, or
  oversized shoulder armor.
- The concept does not authorize retopology, rigging, animation, export, or a
  gate claim before the next direct visual review.
