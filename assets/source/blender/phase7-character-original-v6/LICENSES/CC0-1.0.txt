CC0 purpose relevant to this record
-----------------------------------

CC0 is a public-domain dedication with a public-license fallback. The
canonical legal code describes use, modification, incorporation,
redistribution, and commercial purposes without an attribution condition,
subject to its limitations and disclaimers. Trademark, patent, privacy,
publicity, and third-party rights are not automatically cleared by CC0.
