# Character refinement v2 visual self-review

Status: **candidate for product-owner direction review — not approved**

The blocky v1 benchmark remains rejected. This separate v2 lane is a concrete
response, not an attempt to relabel that benchmark.

## What improved

- Human proportions now come from a contoured body and tapered limb surfaces.
- Armor follows the body through bowed/tapered plates and deliberate curved
  shells; soft goods remain visible at the waist, joints, neck, and arms.
- Gloves have palms, wrist transitions, four modeled fingers, and thumbs.
- The rifle is continuous and the dedicated contact view exposes both the
  pistol-grip and handguard relationships.
- Color has functional hierarchy, while grayscale captures preserve the large
  form and left/right asymmetry.
- Front, side, and back checks expose silhouette and attachment relationships
  instead of relying on one flattering view.

## What still reads as procedural

- The face is intentionally helmeted and simplified; it does not establish a
  production-quality human head.
- Some tertiary surfaces are geometry-only and lack authored texture detail,
  wear, stitching, or normal-map response.
- Rigid-part joints cannot demonstrate soft deformation, cloth compression, or
  believable skin/armor interaction in motion.
- The one combat-ready pose and subtle breathe loop do not prove locomotion,
  recoil, reload, traversal, damage, or first-person presentation.

This candidate should be judged as a new visual-direction checkpoint. A human
review may accept the direction, request another refinement, or reject it; the
files do not encode that decision themselves.
