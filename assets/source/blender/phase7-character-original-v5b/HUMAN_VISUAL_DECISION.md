# KYX Vanguard original v5b visual decision

- Decision: **REJECTED — ORIGINAL_V5B_VISUAL_REJECTED**
- G6: **not claimed**
- Runtime integration: **not authorized**
- Review basis: direct inspection of the generated hero, front, side, back, grayscale, weapon-contact, and trigger-contact plates on 2026-07-21.

## What genuinely improved over rejected v5

- The voxel-inflated body was replaced with explicitly authored torso and limb section lofts.
- Pelvis, thigh, calf, forearm, and upper-arm mass is materially more restrained and adult-proportioned.
- The rifle receiver, handguard, rail, optic, grip, and magazine are slimmer.
- The support hand was moved forward by approximately 0.37 m in the rifle design frame.
- Both hands use fitted profiled palms and separate tapered phalanx objects rather than ball palms with decorative dot fingers.
- The clean-room originality boundary, skin, action, concept digest, GLB, source `.blend`, and render packet are structurally intact.

## Why it is still rejected

- The overall result still reads as a procedural hard-surface mannequin rather than a production character.
- Helmet reads as a dome plus flat jaw plate; it lacks a genuinely authored brow, crown, cheek, rear-shell, and seal transition.
- Torso and limb anatomy remains capsule/tube-like with blank garment surfaces and insufficient shoulder, elbow, pelvis, knee, and ankle landmarks.
- Armor reads as floating shell plates; shoulder and knee pieces remain slab/clamshell forms.
- Boots read as shallow ski-like extrusions rather than a developed outsole, heel, ankle, toe, and shin system.
- Rifle still reads primarily as block/rod extrusion construction despite the slimmer dimensions.
- Trigger close-up does not make an index fingertip resting on the modeled trigger unmistakable; it reads as contact with the outside/guard region.
- Support grip remains open and robotic rather than a convincing palm/finger wrap under load.

## Required next source pipeline

Do not continue polishing this procedural base as though it can pass G6. The next canonical source needs one of these provenance-safe starting points:

1. a genuinely sculpted original humanoid created by a character artist from the pinned model sheet, then retopologized and UV-authored; or
2. a high-quality original base mesh with explicit, release-compatible provenance and transformation rights, followed by substantial project-original sculpt, garment, armor, helmet, glove, boot, and rifle authorship.

That source must then receive a canonical deformation rig, production weights, LOD0/1/2, first-person arms, benchmark locomotion/combat clips, and contact-verified firing/support poses. Neutral and grayscale review plates must prove silhouette and construction before runtime integration. Counts, hashes, exporter success, and generated metadata cannot reverse this visual rejection.
