# Provenance

## Authorship and parentage

The v2 source was generated by `build_press_hall_final_art_v2.py` from the exact verified v1 Blender source. The parent v1 SHA-256 is `e1d186c630bd87ee6c68b58427ba095086bae57bcf4daa147afbd859d7f92495`.

All v2 delta geometry, materials, lighting, cameras, renders, and boards were authored for this repository with deterministic Blender Python. No third-party mesh, texture, scan, font, logo, marketplace asset, or copied topology is included. Materials are original procedural node graphs. The v2 delta removes eight unsupported v1 hanging-sheet objects and replaces them with attached rollers, housings, cables, folded stock, press machinery, fasteners, conduit runs, load framing, wear marks, and restrained safety-registration elements.

The non-binding v1 concept image supplied broad material and landmark direction only. It was not traced into geometry or collision.
