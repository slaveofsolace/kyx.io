# Strict self-review: Press Hall final-art candidate v1

Decision: **retain as a bounded G5 art candidate; do not pass G5**.

## What cleared this authoring strike

- The gameplay and far renders read as one coherent industrial arena rather than a whitebox or a random primitive field.
- The split reactor, asymmetric press baffles, tiled floor shell, wall cassettes, overhead paper, and Red Fold create room-level shape hierarchy before surface detail.
- Cover is no longer presented as undecorated cubes: each locked cover envelope contains an angled manufactured silhouette, nested ceramic armor, an ink value break, and a sparse vermilion cut.
- The Red Fold has a distinct split-ring silhouette and paired upper/lower pad language, so its interaction identity is not carried by color alone.
- The grayscale gameplay render preserves pale route surfaces, dark structural recesses, cover edges, and the reactor silhouette.
- All ten revision-2 replacement groups remain inside their immutable envelopes; the staged GLB contains no authority-collision role.

## Visible weaknesses that remain

- Paper Drop is present and identifiable through its frame, suspended sheets, landing mark, and garden seam, but its dedicated camera is materially less expressive than the concept target. The dark opening and neighboring reactor dominate the shot.
- The environment has a deliberately clean manufactured finish. Secondary wear, surface breakup, and edge history are not yet at final shipping density.
- The split reactor is visually coherent, but its close view exposes repeated panel cadence. A later retained pass can add bounded, non-colliding functional asymmetry without changing the envelope.
- Static Blender review cannot prove visibility under gameplay motion, combat effects, team colors, accessibility modes, or the shipping post-processing stack.
- No runtime triangle, draw-call, material-slot, shader, memory, frame-time, or occlusion budget has been accepted.
- No in-engine capsule/no-snag traversal or human combat/readability playtest has been performed against this render shell.

## Required before any G5 claim

1. Integrate this GLB only through an explicit staged art-preview binding while revision 1 remains default.
2. Re-run locked revision-2 authority movement and no-snag tapes with the render shell loaded independently of collision.
3. Capture deterministic in-engine gameplay, close, mid, far, grayscale, and color-vision views.
4. Measure GPU/CPU frame time, draw calls, material count, triangles, memory, and loading behavior on the target profile.
5. Conduct human visual review and representative combat/readability playtests.
6. Either improve Paper Drop hierarchy or explicitly accept its current supporting-landmark role.

Non-claims: `G5_NOT_PASSED`, `HUMAN_VISUAL_ACCEPTANCE_NOT_PASSED`, `PERFORMANCE_NOT_MEASURED`, `PRODUCT_INTEGRATION_NOT_COMPLETE`, and `REVISION_2_NOT_DEFAULT_OR_SHIPPING`.
