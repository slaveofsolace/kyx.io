# Provenance

## Authorship

All mesh geometry in `inkfall_foundry_press_hall_final_art_v1.blend` was authored for this repository by `build_press_hall_final_art_v1.py`. The build uses Blender primitives only as construction operations; the delivered forms are composed and transformed into custom panel profiles, annular sector meshes, split-ring transition pieces, manufactured edge treatments, layered wall cassettes, reactor shells, baffles, covers, and a garden/conduit seam.

No third-party mesh, texture, scan, font, logo, or marketplace asset is included. Materials are original procedural Blender node materials with stable semantic P6.7 slots.
