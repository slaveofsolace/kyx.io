## Modeling requirements extracted from the sheet

- Preserve athletic human anatomy and intentional taper through shoulders, elbows, wrists, pelvis, knees, calves, and ankles.
- Treat cloth as continuous garment surfaces with folds and tension, then layer a limited number of integrated ceramic shells over it.
- Build the helmet as brow, cheek/face seal, shell, rear panel, and flexible neck layers rather than one rounded primitive.
- Give gloves a shaped palm, thumb web, knuckles, and individually readable grip contact.
- Give boots a real outsole, heel, toe volume, ankle articulation, and shin interface.
- Model the rifle as a unified authored receiver with clear stock, grip, magazine, barrel, and muzzle—not floating rectangular parts.
- Keep one asymmetrical silhouette feature and 3–5 large value masses; avoid decorative greeble density.
- Match construction consistently across front, side, back, and action views while resolving any image-generation ambiguity through functional design judgment.
