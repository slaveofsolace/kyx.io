accepted first-person model.

## Observed result

The VLR-7 occupies less of the target cone and has more top, side, handguard,
rail, and muzzle information than v12. The contact rig is present, but its
dominant forearm projects as one nearly vertical tube beneath the receiver. The
support arm is mostly occluded by the gun and does not communicate a convincing
forward grip. The result is mechanically better than the arm-free block, but it
does not yet look like a natural two-hand rifle pose.

the support forearm toward the handguard, reduce sleeve radius, and retain both
