# Open-mid sniper-shotgun counterplay notes

Status: packet complete for review; **G5 remains open**.

## Intended play

Press mid now supports a long-range read without removing close-range agency:

- west-to-east sniper lane: 28,018 mm and clear at 1,600 mm eye height;
- east-to-west sniper lane: 28,018 mm and clear at 1,600 mm eye height;
- west shotgun breach: traversable with zero automated snags;
- east shotgun breach: traversable with zero automated snags.

## Retained counters

- West inner baffle blocks the designated 6,000 mm west counter-line.
- East inner baffle blocks the designated 6,000 mm east counter-line.
- North reactor blocks the designated 18,000 mm power-position line.
- Outer baffles, side routes, elevation changes, and reactor mass remain
  available to break aim and compress distance.
- The baffles are shorter, not removed: their endpoints create commitment
  points where a shotgun player can stage, shoulder-peek, or punish a sniper
  who overextends.

## Expected rhythm

1. Snipers gain a readable first-look lane through the opened center.
2. Opponents can disappear behind baffle/reactor cover rather than remaining
   continuously exposed.
3. Shotgun players can take either symmetric breach route and force a
   close-range decision.
4. The sniper must reposition, cede space, or correctly predict the breach.

## Human questions

- Is either sniper direction materially easier to hold?
- Can a shotgun player cross one cover gap without taking unavoidable damage?
- Do baffle endpoints create useful mind games or merely hard camping corners?
- Is the reactor counter-line strong enough to prevent a single dominant
  north power position?
- Do 4- and 8-player crossfires turn the open center into a no-go zone?
- Are spawn exits readable before a sniper can acquire them?
- Does the interaction feel fun after side and weapon swaps, not only during
  first exposure?

## Failure signals

- one sniper can watch both breach exits without repositioning;
- a spawn-to-mid path is exposed before the player gains control;
- shotgun players must cross the entire 28 m lane without cover;
- baffle corners produce repeated unavoidable close-range deaths;
- 8-player crossfire eliminates viable rotation;
- players avoid mid despite the intended sniper-shotgun contest.

Any failure signal reopens graybox geometry. Automated sightline success alone
cannot close it.
